# Reference justifications

Workspace rule: cited references must be at most 10 years old, and most should be from the last
5 years. A reference that is 6–10 years old may be cited only with a strict justification here
(checked by `./ra verify-bib`). References older than 10 years may not be cited at all.

Format (one section per bib key):

    ## <key> (<year>)
    - Essential because: <why the paper cannot make its point without this source; what exactly is used>
    - No recent alternative because: <which recent sources were searched and why none can replace it>
